# Poet portrait generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each section contains the complete final prompt used for its selected image. Hafez and Nezami were each regenerated once after inspection. All other portraits used their first generated result. Every portrait was visually inspected for text, extra colours, shading, frames, turbans, religious symbols, stroke consistency and framing. Selected source PNGs were copied into this folder and resized from 1254×1254 to 1024×1024 with sips.

Style reference for every image after Hafez: the final `poet-hafez.png` portrait (the original full-resolution generated file was supplied to the tool). This is a style reference, not a photographic likeness reference.

## poet-hafez.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE portrait for a playful Persian poetry-game app, readable inside a 60–90 px white sticker UI. Output a single 1024x1024 square PNG.
FULL SHARED STYLE BLOCK — identical for all sixteen portraits:
- Pure line art: one single, even stroke weight, about 7 px at 1024 px, rounded line caps, colour ink #14112B only.
- Background pure white #FFFFFF, no fill, no shading, no hatching, no gradients, no colour beyond the specified ink, no texture. Hair, beards, eyes, glasses and clothing must remain white inside with sparse outline strokes only; NEVER solid dark filled areas.
- Head and shoulders, centred, face filling about 60% of the canvas, small margin all around.
- Modern, friendly editorial line drawing, simple and confident, slightly stylised (think one-line portrait illustration), not a caricature, not photorealistic, minimal detail but the person must stay recognisable.
- Same framing, scale, stroke and level of detail across the whole set, so they look like one family. Use head crown near y=100, eyes near y=420, chin around y=710 (beard may reach y=790), shoulders down to y=940. Centre the face at x=512. Keep the entire silhouette inside the canvas with white margins. Sparse facial features and a few flowing hair/beard contours, never dense parallel strokes.
- 1024x1024 square PNG.
- Absolutely no text, letters, names, numbers, signature or watermark. No frame, circle or border.
FULL SHARED HARD RULES:
- No religious symbols of any kind: no clerical turbans, no prayer beads, no crescents, no stars, no halos, no mosque or dome shapes, no calligraphy.
- Classical (pre-modern) poets: show them bare-headed or with a simple soft felt cap or a plain shawl over the shoulders. Never a turban. For this set choose BARE-HEADED for every classical poet, with visible natural hair, no headwear.
- Modern poets: base the likeness on their well-known photographs, translated into the exact same sparse outline-only editorial drawing style.
Do not draw a sticker circle; the application will supply it. No props unless explicitly specified in the subject cues. No text of any kind.
SUBJECT FOR THIS IMAGE ONLY: Hafez. Classical Persian poet. Middle-aged man, full rounded beard, gentle half-smile, calm dreamy eyes. Bare-headed, visible natural hair.
STRICT MONOLINE EXECUTION: Every mark must be the SAME constant 7 px width, like a round technical pen; NO tapered brush strokes, NO thick eyebrows, NO black pupils, NO solid filled shapes. Eyebrows are single thin arcs, pupils are tiny open circles, moustaches are open outline shapes. Draw straight-on, symmetric head-and-shoulders framing. Respect specified margins and sparse simplicity. Pure flat white background, no paper simulation.
```
