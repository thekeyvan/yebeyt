# yebeyt · poet portraits, round 1

Goal: a consistent set of LINE-ART portraits of famous Persian poets for a playful, Gen Z poetry-game app (sticker-style UI on light background). Each portrait will sit inside a small white circle sticker (60–90 px on screen), so it must read clearly when small.

## Style (identical for every image)
- Pure line art: one single, even stroke weight (about 7 px at 1024 px), rounded line caps, colour ink #14112B only.
- Background pure white #FFFFFF, no fill, no shading, no hatching, no gradients, no colour, no texture.
- Head and shoulders, centred, face filling about 60% of the canvas, small margin all around.
- Modern, friendly editorial line drawing, simple and confident, slightly stylised (think one-line portrait illustration), not a caricature, not photorealistic, minimal detail but the person must stay recognisable.
- Same framing, scale, stroke and level of detail across the whole set, so they look like one family.
- 1024x1024 square PNG.
- Absolutely no text, letters, names, numbers, signature or watermark. No frame, circle or border.

## Hard rules
- No religious symbols of any kind: no clerical turbans, no prayer beads, no crescents, no stars, no halos, no mosque or dome shapes, no calligraphy.
- Classical (pre-modern) poets: show them bare-headed or with a simple soft felt cap or a plain shawl over the shoulders. Never a turban.
- Modern poets: base the likeness on their well-known photographs.
