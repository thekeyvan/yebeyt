# yebeyt icon — round 4: Gen Z

History: round 1 neon outlines = cheap. Round 2 Apple glass = good quality but generic. Round 3 Persian calligraphy, tazhib, divan book = "all very bad". The user now says: **no Persian letters needed; aim the concept at Gen Z.**

What the app is, for a Gen Z user: a daily swipe deck of beautiful short poems, sending a couplet to a friend like a story/postcard, a two-person fortune ("fal") with a friend, and small word games with friends. Calm, aesthetic, a bit mystical (moon, night, fortune), social in a small-circle way.

Shared style (every image):
- Looks like a top-chart Gen Z app icon designed by Apple's own icon team: one bold, simple, instantly memorable mark. Confident, playful, a little cheeky, never corporate, never old-fashioned, never "cultural heritage", no ornaments, no books, no scrolls, no pens, no letters or text of any script.
- Big shapes: the mark fills about 60–70% of the canvas, centred with optical balance. Must read at 40px.
- Rich but simple rendering: smooth soft-3D/clay or glossy glass volume, crisp highlight from the top, soft contact shadow. Perfectly smooth, no grain, no noise, no scattered background stars, no vignette.
- Palette: dark background (deep ink-navy #0B1426 to deep teal #0E3A44) with turquoise #2EC4D6 as the hero colour; electric tints #5FF2FF and #C9F6FA allowed; ONE small pop accent allowed (soft lilac #B79CFF or warm peach #FFB38A) if it helps.
- Square 1024×1024, full-bleed, do NOT draw rounded corners or a frame around the image, no device or mockup.

## A — sleepy moon mascot
A cute minimal mascot: a chubby turquoise crescent moon character with two closed, content curved eyes and tiny blush, slight smug smile, soft clay-3D. Tiny peach star tucked in its curve. Kawaii but cool, like a sticker Gen Z would put on a laptop. No other elements.

## B — the swipe deck
Three rounded cards fanned out like a hand of cards being swiped, front card bright turquoise with a single small white sparkle (four-point star) in its centre, the cards behind in deeper teal and lilac. Bold, glossy, playful tilt, feels like "swipe for today's drop".

## C — two bubbles, one spark
Two chunky rounded speech-bubble blobs overlapping, one turquoise and one lilac, their overlap glowing brighter, and a small white sparkle where they touch. Means two friends trading a line. Soft-3D, glossy, very simple.

## D — the fortune eye
A bold, friendly stylised eye shape (almond) made of turquoise glass, its pupil a small glowing crescent moon, three tiny lash ticks on top. Mystical-cool, tarot/astrology energy that Gen Z loves, but clean and graphic.

## E — moon + spark mark
An ultra-simple bold logo mark: a thick, perfectly geometric turquoise crescent with a single chunky four-point white sparkle nestled in its opening, slight glossy volume. As iconic and minimal as the best social-app logos.

## F — the drop
A glossy turquoise rounded square tile (like a single message or story card) with a small speech-bubble tail at the bottom-left, slightly tilted, a tiny lilac heart-shaped sparkle popping off its top-right corner. Fun, tactile, sticker-like.
