# yebeyt icon — round 2: "as if Apple designed it"

Round 1 was rejected: neon glow lines on a flat dark vignette looked cheap and amateur. Do NOT make neon, outline-only, glowing-line, or clip-art icons.

Target: an icon that could sit next to Apple's own first-party iOS 26 icons (Books, Notes, Music, Photos, Wallet, Journal) in their **Liquid Glass / layered** icon language:
- One simple, bold, solid central symbol, centered with optical balance, about 55–65% of the canvas width, generous even margins.
- Built from 2–3 stacked layers of real-feeling material: frosted translucent glass with a crisp specular rim light along the top edges, subtle refraction where layers overlap, soft realistic contact shadow beneath. Light comes from the top.
- Smooth, rich background gradient (no vignette, no noise, no stars scattered around): deep ink-navy #0B1426 at the bottom to deep teal #0E3A44 at the top.
- Main colour: Persian turquoise #2EC4D6, with lighter tints #7FE3EC–#C9F6FA in highlights and deeper #128A9A in shadows. Small touch of warm white allowed for highlights.
- Clean, premium, minimal, calm. Studio-quality 3D render, perfectly smooth surfaces, no grain.
- Square 1024×1024, full-bleed background, do NOT draw rounded corners or a border (iOS applies the mask), no text, no Latin letters, no UI chrome, no mockup, no device, no shadow outside the square.

## A — glass letter ب
A single thick, solid Persian letter "ب" (beh) in a smooth calligraphic naskh shape: a wide, gently curved horizontal bowl with a short upturned tip on the right and a smaller upturn on the left, and ONE dot centered below it. The letter is a chunky piece of frosted turquoise glass with rounded edges and a bright specular rim on its top edge. The dot below is a small polished glass sphere that glows softly like a star. The letter floats slightly above the background with a soft shadow. Correct Persian letterform, nothing else.

## B — two glass cards (a couplet sent between two people)
Two rounded-rectangle glass cards, portrait orientation, stacked: a back card in deeper translucent teal, tilted about 8° left; a front card in frosted pale turquoise glass, tilted about 4° right, slightly overlapping. On the front card, a simple solid white crescent moon is embossed in the centre. The cards look like real thick glass tiles with bevelled edges, specular top rim, and refraction where they overlap. Feels like a postcard, a playing card and a page at once.

## C — open book with a crescent
A simple open book seen slightly from above, its two pages fanning upward in a soft V, made of frosted turquoise glass with white-ish inner pages. Floating just above the spine, a small solid polished crescent moon in warm white-turquoise. Bold silhouette, readable at small size, Apple Books-level simplicity.

## D — speech bubble with a couplet
A single rounded speech bubble made of thick frosted turquoise glass. Inside it, two short horizontal rounded bars side by side with a small gap between them (like the two half-lines of a Persian couplet), in solid white, slightly raised. A tiny glass star sits at the bubble's top-right edge. Simple, iconic, like an Apple first-party app.
