# yebeyt icon — round 8: refine the two favourites

The user liked round 7's **#1 (Simurgh feather as a quill)** and **#2 (Simurgh whose wings are book pages)** — both attached as references (first image = feather quill, second = page-wings). Refine them: same matte finish and palette, cleaner, more iconic, better at small sizes. Use the shared rules in shared.md for every image.

Known issues to fix:
- Feather quill: composition sat in a thin diagonal with empty corners; nib is a Western fountain-pen nib.
- Page-wings: the bird's head looked like a phoenix/eagle (hooked beak, fierce brow); "book with wings" risks a library-logo cliché; too many thin page layers.
- For both: the Simurgh must feel gentle and mythic Persian (long curling crest, soft small beak, calm eye), never fierce.

## A1 — feather quill, reed-pen nib
The Simurgh feather from reference 1, larger and bolder, set at about 40° so it fills the square well. Its lower end is cut like a Persian reed pen (qalam): a clean angled cut in warm natural reed tone with a fine slit, instead of a metal nib. One small turquoise ink drop below. Feather vane: turquoise into lilac with the peach curled tip. Fewer, larger feather layers (3–4).

## A2 — feather quill, gold nib, simplified
Same as reference 1 (gold nib kept) but simplified: 3 big feather layers, a single elegant peach curl at the top, larger overall and better centred, the ink drop slightly bigger so it reads at small size.

## A3 — feather writing a line
The Simurgh feather quill (reed-cut tip) at an angle, its tip drawing one continuous, flowing turquoise-to-lilac ribbon line that sweeps across the lower part of the icon in a graceful wave, like a line of verse being written, ending in a small curl. No letters, just the ribbon line.

## B1 — page-wings, gentle Simurgh
The composition of reference 2, refined: the Simurgh's head is gentle and mythic, with a long curling turquoise crest, small soft peach beak, calm closed-ish eye, no eagle brow. Each wing is 3 broad book pages (not many thin ones), white-turquoise with subtle lilac edges. Lilac and peach ribbon tail curls below. Compact, symmetrical.

## B2 — page-wings, book first
An open book seen from the front, its pages lifting and curving upward on both sides so they become the two wings of a Simurgh whose slim turquoise neck and crested head rise from the spine at the centre. The book is the base and larger; the bird is smaller and elegant. No tail.

## B3 — hybrid: page-wings with quill tail
The gentle Simurgh of B1 with page-wings, but its tail is a single long Simurgh feather that ends in a reed-pen cut tip, with a tiny ink drop. Combines both favourites. Keep it simple enough to read at small size.
