# yebeyt icon — round 6: same Iranian-culture ideas, less jelly

Round 5 (attached reference, gol-o-bolbol) had the right ideas, but the user says the render is "a bit too jelly": too glossy, candy-like, wobbly, plastic. Keep the ideas and the palette; change the finish to something more refined and grown-up, like Apple's own iOS 26 icons.

ABSOLUTE RULE — NO RELIGION, NO POLITICS. None of: crescent moons, moon+star, Faravahar or winged discs, mosque domes/minarets/arches, crosses, Star of David, prayer hands, religious calligraphy, Lion and Sun, flags, crowns. No letters or text of any script.

Shared rules (every image):
- NOT jelly, NOT gummy, NOT candy, NOT inflated balloon, NOT wet-glossy plastic. No big white specular blobs.
- Shapes are crisp and intentional: clean geometric curves, precise edges, consistent stroke-free silhouettes, like a designer drew them with vector curves first.
- Background: deep ink navy #070F22 to #0B1426, smooth, no vignette, no scattered stars, no noise.
- Palette: turquoise #2EC4D6 hero (tints #5FF2FF, #C9F6FA, shade #128A9A), soft lilac #B79CFF and warm peach #FFB38A accents, tiny soft gold allowed.
- One simple, memorable silhouette, centred with optical balance, 60–70% of the canvas, reads at 40px. Gen Z-friendly but elegant.
- Square 1024×1024, full-bleed, do NOT draw rounded corners or a frame, no device or mockup.

Finish "MATTE": soft-touch matte 3D, like ceramic or anodised aluminium, gentle diffuse studio light from the top, subtle soft gradients across each surface, a thin crisp rim highlight on the top edges only, soft contact shadow. Very slight depth, closer to Apple's iOS 26 icons than to a toy.

Finish "FLAT": bold flat vector graphic, 3–4 solid colour areas with at most one subtle gradient, very slight inner shading for depth, no gloss, no 3D render. Like a top-tier modern brand mark.
