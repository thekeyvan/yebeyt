# yebeyt app icon brief

App: **yebeyt** — home-screen name «یه بیت» ("one couplet"). An iOS app that is:
1. A large library of Persian poets and poems (Hafez, Rumi, Saadi, Ferdowsi… from Ganjoor).
2. A calm, small-circle social network: send a couplet to a friend like a postcard, a shared fal (Hafez divination) for two, shared diwans. No public feed, no likes.
3. Games inside: مشاعره (poetry duel: reply with a couplet starting with the last letter of the previous one), guess the next hemistich, drag words into place.

Brand look (already settled — keep to it):
- Dark, cinematic, calm. Deep navy/ink to dark teal background with soft glow.
- Accent: Persian turquoise #2EC4D6 (فیروزه‌ای). Light tints toward #9FF0F7.
- "Letter-art": scenes drawn from Persian letters; a crescent moon and small stars recur.
- Light, elegant strokes; never heavy bold type; never busy.

Icon rules:
- iOS app icon, 1024×1024, square, full-bleed background, NO rounded corners drawn (iOS masks it), no transparency.
- ONE strong idea, readable at 60px on a home screen. No small text, no Latin letters, no words.
- Persian letterforms must be correct (right-to-left, real نستعلیق/naskh shapes), not fake squiggles.
- Not a generic crescent; it should say "Persian poetry + two people sharing a couplet + a little play".

Concepts to generate (one image each):
- **A — ب with a star dot**: a single calligraphic Persian letter ب (first letter of بیت) in glowing turquoise nastaliq; its bowl curves like a crescent/boat and its dot below is a bright star. Minimal.
- **B — two hemistichs**: a couplet is two half-lines (مصراع). Two elegant calligraphic strokes side by side, mirrored, that together form the outline of a speech bubble / open book. Implies sharing and conversation.
- **C — turquoise tile**: a Persian girih 8-point star like an Isfahan turquoise dome tile, softly glowing, with ب at its center; the star reads a little like a game piece.
- **D — couplet card**: a small glowing card (like a postcard or playing card) tilted slightly, with an abstract letter-art crescent made of Persian letters on it, a second card peeking behind it (two people, a game deck).
