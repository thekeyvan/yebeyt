# yebeyt 2.0 · what we are building

Many look-alike Persian poetry apps launched in 2026. yebeyt 2.0 sets itself apart with a playful personality: games, a small circle of friends, and messages, around a calm reader. The design was settled screen by screen with the owner; this file is the decision record for building it. Anything not written here or on a board is still open: ask the user, don't invent.

It is a new app built from scratch, in a monorepo (layout in `AGENTS.md`). Nothing from the earlier 1.0 code or its server is reused and there is no earlier user data to migrate; 1.0 was never distributed. 2.0 ships as the same App Store app (`com.yebeyt.app`). iOS comes first; the web app, admin dashboard and Android come after the iOS slices and are not designed yet.

## Look: stickers

The full rules are on the `StickerSystem` board. In short:

- Colours: lapis `#2F35F5` (main), fire `#FF4D24` (streak, competition), amber `#FFB703` (points), green `#6CCB3A` (correct), magenta `#FF3D8B` (friends, poets), ink `#14112B` (text), paper `#F1EFEA` with dots `#D9D5CE` (background), white `#FFFFFF`.
- Stickers have a white border (3–5 pt) and a soft shadow (about `0 3 10` at 18% ink). No ink outlines.
- Cards, buttons, badges and text are always straight. Only decorative stickers tilt 5–12°, two or three per screen at most. One peeled corner per screen at most, on a big card. A sticker you are holding is bigger, tilted, with a longer shadow.
- Dotted paper background, like a sticker sheet, on every tab including home (the owner chose this over the plain home background on the `Calm` board).
- Fonts: titles **Lalezar**, interface **Vazirmatn**, poems **Parastoo Bold** (the default poem font). The text sheet also offers regular Parastoo. All four live in `assets/fonts/` with their OFL licences.
- App icon: for now the two-sticker mark from `StickerSystem` (drawn by `tools/make_icon.swift`).
- Tab bar: the `nav()` function in `design/canvas/gen_explore.py` is its exact spec (white card, raised round «بازی» in the middle, avatar on «من», unread badge on «پیام‌ها»). Until avatars exist (slice 5) «من» shows our own line icon in the same ring.
- Icons are our own (the app's line icons and the game icons in `design/canvas/gameicons.py`: round stickers, white border, one colour per game). No SF Symbols in the visible design.
- Tabs: خونه · کاوش · بازی · پیام‌ها · من.

**Motion.** The owner asked for livelier, Duolingo-like animation (24 Nov). One vocabulary in `apps/ios/Yebeyt/Design/Motion.swift` (numbers), `Burst.swift` (the pure piece generator) and `MotionEffects.swift` (the views), all under 0.5 s and never blocking a tap:
- `bouncy`: spring, response 0.35, damping 0.55 (visible overshoot). Toasts drop in with it (`Motion.toast`); the next deck card springs in with it.
- `pop`: scale 1 → 1.35 → 0.9 → 1.05 → 1 over 0.45 s; stickers also wiggle ±8°. Turning a mark off pops smaller (1.15, 0.3 s, no wiggle, no particles).
- `press`: every sticker button squishes to 0.92 while pressed and springs back (response 0.3, damping 0.5): `PressStyle` (`.buttonStyle(.press)`) and `pressSquish` inside the sticker button styles.
- `burst`: the pieces and the icon's own move belong to the action (the owner wanted pop-ups related to the icon, 24 Nov), in sticker colours, fading in 0.6 s. No crescents or stars. A **like** pops and 4–6 small hearts (fire, pink) float up with a sideways drift; a **keep** drops the bookmark in from above with a bounce and 3–4 small lapis ribbons fan out upward; a **repost** spins its arrows once around and 2 small lapis arrows circle out; a **send** flies the plane off toward its nose (the top corner it points to; the icon is never mirrored) and lands back, with no particles; a **right answer** pops 4–6 small green ticks; a **won round** (round end, the daily puzzle solved) keeps the confetti of dots, short dashes and small squares (radius 74 pt). A wrong answer has no particles, only the shake. Taking a like, keep or repost off only pops small.
- `shake`: a wrong answer shakes ±14 pt over 0.45 s.
- Haptics (`.sensoryFeedback`): medium impact on keep, like and repost (not on taking them off; none on a send), success on a right answer and on a won or good round (a win, or at least half right), error on a wrong answer, selection when the person taps another tab. The particles and the tap of a like, keep or repost come only from a tap on that control (`MarkButton`); a change from elsewhere (sync, the same couplet in another view) at most pops small.
- Used on: keep, like, repost and send everywhere (reader row, hold menu, whole-poem bar, home card, feed, shared divan, answer sheet): the action's icon move and particles, impact; «رد کن» on home throws the card off to the side with a tilt (it springs back if there is no other card) and the next springs in; the answer sheet's verdict badge pops with ticks and a success tap (a confetti burst for the daily puzzle solved) or shakes with an error tap; the right option pops and a wrong chosen one shakes; matched معنی‌یاب cards pop; right daily letters pop; the score sticker pops and the end-of-round score and points count up, and the score gets a big confetti burst and a success tap only for a win (a duel or challenge won) or a solo round with at least half right, otherwise a plain pop; the selected tab's icon pops.
- **Reduce Motion** (`Motion.variant(reduceMotion:)`): no particles, no wiggle, no spin or fly, no shake and no thrown card (it fades); a pop becomes a 0.2 s scale to 1.05; the press squish shrinks to 0.97 without overshoot; the count-up jumps; haptics stay.
- **Sheets, tabs, covers, menus and rows** (the owner, 24 Nov: «bottom sheets don't feel like bottom sheets», «I still see fade animations»). Fading is only for Reduce Motion (a plain crossfade, `Motion.rises`); everything else moves with `bouncy` and goes away quickly (`Motion.drop`, no overshoot). All in `Motion.swift`:
  - A **sheet** is a normal iPhone bottom sheet: native `.sheet` wherever nothing prevents it, with a visible grabber, the 30 pt corners and a drag down that closes it (`stickerSheet()` in `SheetStyle.swift`; `fitsSheetHeight()` for the height of its content). The only sheet that refuses the drag is the feedback one while something unsent is written: it rubber-bands and then asks (`interactiveDismissDisabled`).
  - A sheet we draw ourselves (the answer sheet after a game answer: its particles and the AI note must leave the sheet's frame, which a native sheet would clip) rises from the bottom with `bouncy` (`Motion.sheet`) while its dimmed background fades in (`Motion.scrim`), and follows the finger (`dragToDismiss`, `SheetDrag.swift`): down follows 1:1, up rubber-bands (at most 40 pt); letting go past 30% of its height, or flicking down at 800 pt/s or more, closes it, anything less springs back with `bouncy`. What «closing» means is decided per sheet: the answer sheet's close is «سؤال بعدی» (the same as its big button; «برگرد» in the daily puzzle), so a drag does that. A tap on its dimmed background does nothing there (a stray tap would skip the verdict).
  - The **tab** page slides in 40 pt from the side of the tapped tab (mirrored in right-to-left: کاوش is to the left of خونه, so its page comes from the left) while the page that leaves slides 40 pt out to the far side, both with `bouncy` and only a short secondary fade (`Motion.tab(incoming:outgoing:)`, `departure`). Tab switches made in code (opening a poet, a chat, launch routes) don't know the direction, so their old page only fades.
  - **Covers** (the reader, a game) are bottom sheets that fill the screen: ONE layer (`CoverLayer`: all of it, background, chrome and content, moves with a single offset) rises from the bottom edge with a firm spring (damping 0.9, so it settles without going past the top; `bouncy` is for small things), no scale, and drops quickly (`Motion.coverOffset`, `coverRise`, `coverDrop`); a new cover is first drawn below the edge and rises only once that mounted layer has been drawn (a 34 ms wait inside its `.task`); starting the rise in the same update as the mounting made the background and the content animate separately, which was the "drawn in pieces" bug. Under Reduce Motion a cover crossfades. The reader is an overlay in `RootView` like the game, not a system `fullScreenCover`. The layer stays mounted while it drops, so what closes with it goes down in the same motion (after «برو بیرون» the question and the game leave together). The tab bar stays underneath while a game rises and leaves once the game covers it. The reader's header (with a grabber) is its handle: a drag down on it follows the finger and closes by the usual 30% / 800 pt/s rule; its content keeps its own scrolling and paging.
  - **Tabs inside a page** (`SegmentedControl`): the highlight slides to the chosen option with `bouncy`, and the page the control swaps (`segmentPage`, with a shared `SegmentPaging`: the section's sort, the divan's grouping, the board's tabs) slides like the tab pages: the new one in from its segment's side, the old one out the other way (`Motion.pageArrival`), a crossfade only under Reduce Motion.
  - **Menus and cards** that appear (the hold menu, an onboarding step) scale up from 0.9 with `bouncy` from where they belong (`Motion.menu`).
  - **List rows** that come or go (a divan couplet taken out or brought back, a friend request accepted or turned down, a chat message that arrives or is sent) scale from 0.9 and slide 12 pt with `bouncy` (`Motion.row`); a chat's first load and older pages do not animate.
  - Kept on purpose: a toast fades when it goes (it already drops in); the count-up and the Reduce Motion press squish use a short ease (numbers and a tiny scale are not movement of a surface); the `.snappy` springs of small switches and scrolls.
- «نگه دار» on the home card keeps in place (it does not throw the card away), so the bookmark drops in and its ribbons fan out instead.

## Decisions by area

**Accounts (row ۱).** No account is needed to read, browse, search, open a fal, listen, play solo games or keep couplets on the phone. Scores, the leaderboard, streaks, games with friends, messages and sync ask for an account when first used (`AccountGate`: a sheet over the current screen; progress made as a guest carries over). Sign-in is Apple or Google only, plus «فعلاً بدون حساب». After sign-in the user picks a username: `a-z 0-9 _ .`, 3–20 characters, live availability check, suggestions, can be changed every 30 days. Onboarding: welcome → taste (poets and moods) → a 30-second jigsaw → account → username → find friends.

**Reading (row ۳).** One couplet at a time (`Poem`) and the whole poem (`PoemScroll`) share the exact same header and mode toggle; switching modes must not move the header. Long-press on a couplet lifts it (`PoemScrollHold`): icon row پسند / بازنشر / بفرست / نگه دار, then a list (meaning of this couplet, read from here couplet by couplet, play with this couplet, copy text). The «آ» sheet (`PoemText`) has font (Parastoo / Parastoo Bold), weight, hemistich layout (staggered / centred), spacing and theme.

**پسند vs نگه داشتن.** پسند is social: friends see it. نگه داشتن puts the couplet in your divan, a private collection. The divan (`Divan`) has search and grouping by poet, topic or newest. A shared divan with a friend (`SharedDivan`) is opened from the chat header.

**Long names.** Poets have a short display name; full names appear on their own pages. In the Explore poet row the label is a fixed width and wraps to a second line under the name; the avatar always stays a circle. Book titles wrap to two lines.

**Pictures that grow over time** (poet portraits now; later book covers, game art and the like). The phone has a default for every item; for poets it is a sticker circle in the poet's colour with the first letter of their name. The real picture comes from the server (api.yebeyt.com on Railway) and, once loaded, is kept on the phone. The list of pictures is asked for on every launch and every time the app comes back to the front (not twice within a minute), so a new picture appears within moments and without an app update, and with no network the saved or default picture shows. The 16 ready portraits in `design/poets` go only to the server, not into the app. Pictures are uploaded with a script at first, later from the admin dashboard. Slice 2 builds Explore with the defaults; slice 4 adds the picture endpoint and loading them in the app.

**Library (row ۲).** All books (search, continue reading, type chips, grid) → one book (cover, continue, random poem, sections) → a section's list (search, sort by number / last letter / most read; read and in-progress marks) → the poem.

**Games (rows ۴ and ۵).** Nine games, each with its own icon: معمای امروز (missing word from letter stickers, 3 tries, streak), جورچین بیت, حدس شاعر (four faces with names), مصراع دوم, از کدوم کتاب؟ (poet shown, pick the book), معنی‌یاب (match words to meanings), گوش کن (recitation with the text hidden; answers are hemistichs, since most poems have no title), مشاعره and بازی رودررو (with friends). Nothing teaches during the timer; after every answer, right or wrong, a sheet shows the full couplet, a one-line meaning, poet and book, with «کل شعر / نگه دار / بفرست» and a big «بیت بعدی». The round ends with a list of its couplets and an invitation to challenge someone (`ChallengeSend`). Leaderboard: plain rows, medals for 1–3, your own row tinted, no podium. Show scores per face; never "x : y" or "۸ / ۱۰" (they reorder in right-to-left text); write «۸ از ۱۰».

**Playing with friends and links (row ۵).** `GameLobby`: pick a game, a turn time (۳۰ ثانیه / ۲ دقیقه / تا فردا), invite friends in the app or share a link. Links are Universal Links on yebeyt.com: `/c/<code>` challenge, `/i/<username>` invite, `/g/<code>` game. Without the app, the web page offers an App Clip to play at once, the App Store, and a visible code; after install the link is matched on the server by code (deferred deep link), with the code as fallback. No account is needed to accept a challenge; the name is asked at the end.

**Invite friends (row ۷).** Tone presets (رفیقانه / شاعرانه / کوتاه), editable text with a مشاعره opener, the personal link, and an avatar item as a reward when friends join.

**Feedback (row ۸).** Shake the phone to open «می‌خوای چیزی بگی؟» with five types. What each sends:

| Type | Screenshot | Other |
|---|---|---|
| باگ دیدم | yes, with a switch | technical info: screen and couplet, mode, recitation position, text settings, network, account state, recent errors, steps before it; optional screen recording |
| یه جاش اذیتم کرد | yes, switch on by default | screen name |
| یه ایده دارم | switch off by default | — |
| یه بازی پیشنهاد بدم | no | text only |
| خوشم اومد | no | text only |

Technical info is shown as one collapsed row «اطلاعات فنی · ببین چیه», only on bug reports. App version and phone model go with every type, silently. Never send text typed elsewhere, messages, phone number, IP or location. Shake can be turned off in settings. Shipping this needs the App Store privacy label (Diagnostics, Product Interaction) and a clause in the privacy policy (on the new website, slice 9; until then the policy text is kept with the feedback slice).

## Asking before it hurts (lead, 24 Nov, after the owner: «any action that can be dangerous should have a confirmation»)

The rule: **ask first when an action loses progress or can't be undone; for a small action that is easy to take back, act at once and show a toast with «برگردون» (undo) instead of asking; never ask when nothing is lost.** One sticker sheet asks (`ConfirmSheet` in `apps/ios/Yebeyt/Design/`): a title, an optional line, the safe button («نه», «بمون») first (on the right in right-to-left) and the red one that does it.

- Closing a solo game (the × of جورچین, حدس شاعر, مصراع دوم, از کدوم کتاب؟, گوش کن, معنی‌یاب, also when it answers a challenge) asks «از بازی بری بیرون؟» / «نتیجه‌ی این دور ثبت نمی‌شه.» (the best score, the leaderboard and a challenge's result; points already earned stay) / «برو بیرون» · «بمون», only once the round has started (an answer is given or its clock runs) and its result screen isn't showing. The game clock waits while the question is open. «بازی با این بیت/غزل» from the reader over such a round asks the same question. A duel never asks: leaving its screen doesn't forfeit. The onboarding game, the daily puzzle (every try is kept, it resumes) and مشاعره (turns wait on the server) never ask.
- Friend requests: «درخواستت رو پس بگیری؟» (پس بگیر / نه) and «درخواستش رو رد کنی؟» / «بهش خبر نمی‌دیم.» (رد کن / نه), wherever the buttons are (a person's page, the requests list, people search).
- Sign out: «از حسابت بیرون بری؟» with what really happens: the divan is synced, leaves this phone, and comes back when signing in again (بیرون برو / نه). The warning for an unsynced divan still follows when the last sync failed.
- Closing the feedback sheet (× or a swipe down) with typed text, a screenshot with marks or a recording not sent: «بازخوردت هنوز فرستاده نشده. ولش کنی؟» (ول کن / بمون); while something is written a swipe can't close it. An empty form closes at once.
- Un-keeping a couplet from the divan (wherever it is one tap: the reader, the hold menu, the home card, the feed, the divan, the after-answer sheet) asks nothing: «از دیوانت برداشته شد» with «برگردون». The way back keeps it again, so it counts as kept now (the sync's last change wins).
- «حذف فیلم» in the feedback form asks «فیلم پاک بشه؟» (پاک کن / نه). Leaving the face screen with changes not saved asks «چهره‌ات ذخیره نشده. ولش کنی؟» (ول کن / بمون). «رد کن» on today's couplet acts at once and shows «امروز دیگه نشونت نمی‌دیم» with «برگردون». The username hint under the field says «… · تا ۳۰ روز نمی‌شه عوضش کرد.» (no dialog); the edge swipe back can't leave a face with unsaved changes. When the feedback report already reached the server and only a file failed, closing asks «فیلم یا عکست فرستاده نشده. ولش کنی؟». A toast with «برگردون» stays for 5 seconds (restarted by every new toast), and with VoiceOver until dismissed.
- Still ask (the same sheet, or a choice list for report): «این پیام پاک بشه؟» (delete message), «از دیوان مشترک برداشته بشه؟» / «برای {name} هم برداشته می‌شه.» (بردار / نه; the hold menu's «بردار» on a couplet I added to a shared divan), report, block, remove a friend, delete account, «کم آوردی؟ بازی رو می‌بازی.» (resign مشاعره).

## One account per email (owner, 27 Nov: «if they already have an account with this email, they are the same»; «why not save the emails?»)

Signing in with Apple and with Google using the same email gives one account, on the phone and on the web.
- The server keeps the email: on each sign-in identity (`identities.email`, indexed), lower-cased and trimmed, and only when the provider says it is verified (Google `email_verified`, Apple `email_verified`). Apple's private relay address (`is_private_email`) is kept too: it is verified and deliverable once the domain is registered with Apple, but it never matches a Google address. Every sign-in rewrites it; a sign-in with no verified email keeps the old one. It is never logged. `GET /v1/me` gives the account's own email (the newest sign-in method that has one, or null).
- Only an address the provider vouches for joins accounts: every verified Apple address, but a Google address only when it is `@gmail.com` or the token has an `hd` claim (a Workspace domain), Google's own guidance. Other verified Google addresses are stored and never used to join. An account never gets two sign-in methods of the same provider this way (a recycled address is not the same person).
- A sign-in with a provider identity the server hasn't seen, whose email matches an existing account's identity, adds the identity to that account; no new account is made. Two first sign-ins with one email take turns, so they make one account. With several matches (only from before this, or two first sign-ins at the same moment) the one with a username wins, then the oldest.
- An identity whose account is still empty (no username yet) moves to the matching account with a username on its next sign-in, and the empty account is deleted with its sessions. «Empty» means nothing beyond the guest carry-over: progress and the divan are merged into the real account (the larger progress values; the later time of each couplet), feedback reports follow the account; privacy settings, name and face are kept only if the real account has none (the empty account's news choice is dropped). An account with anything else (scores, friends, chats, games, challenges, reactions, leagues) is left alone and both accounts stay. Two accounts that both have a username are never merged by themselves.
- Hide My Email at Apple gives a relay address, so it never matches. A setting to link sign-in methods by hand may come later.
- An account made before this needs one more sign-in with its first method before a second method can join it.
- **News by email is opt-in, off by default** (`users.news_opt_in_at`, null is no; `GET /v1/me` has `newsOptIn`, `PUT /v1/me/news` with `{ optIn }` changes it). The switch «خبرهای یه بیت رو برام ایمیل کن» is in settings, on iOS and on the web (built in a follow-up slice). Every such email has a way to stop it. Mail about the account itself needs no opt-in. Before any email is sent, `yebeyt.com` and `hello@yebeyt.com` must be registered at Apple (owner-todo §4), or relay addresses get nothing.
- The email and the choice go when the account is deleted. The privacy policy (both languages) says what is kept and why.
