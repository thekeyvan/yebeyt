## Persian (fa)

**Name:** یه بیت

**Subtitle:** شعر فارسی، بازی و دوستان

**Promotional text:**
هر روز یه بیت تازه از حافظ، سعدی، مولانا و خیام. بخون، گوش کن، با بیت‌ها بازی کن و با دوستات مشاعره کن.

**Description:**
یه بیت، شعر فارسی رو سبک و روزانه می‌کنه.

هر روز یه بیت
- بیت امروز رو ببین، نگهش دار تو دیوانت یا رد کن تا بعدی بیاد.
- فال حافظ بگیر، یا بیت‌ها رو بر اساس حال‌وهوا پیدا کن: عشق، دلتنگی، امید، بهار…

بخون و گوش کن
- دیوان حافظ، گلستان و بوستان سعدی، مثنوی و دیوان شمس، رباعیات خیام، شاهنامه و ده‌ها کتاب دیگه از بیش از چهل شاعر.
- بیت به بیت بخون یا کل شعر رو، با فونت و اندازه‌ی دلخواه.
- معنی بیت‌ها رو ببین.
- خوانش شعرها رو گوش کن؛ متن همراه صدا جلو می‌ره.

بازی کن
- معمای امروز: کلمه‌ی گم‌شده‌ی بیت رو پیدا کن.
- بیت جورچین: کلمه‌ها رو سر جاشون بچسبون.
- حدس شاعر، مصراع دوم، از کدوم کتاب؟، معنی‌یاب و گوش کن.
- امتیاز جمع کن، رکورد روزانه‌ت رو نگه دار و تو رده‌بندی بالا برو.

با دوستات
- دوستات رو پیدا کن، بیت بفرست و پیام بده.
- مشاعره کن، دوئل بازی کن یا یه چالش بفرست.
- دیوان مشترک بساز: بیت‌هایی که با هم دوست دارین.

خوندن، کتابخونه، فال و بازی‌های تکی بدون حساب کار می‌کنن. برای امتیاز، دوستان و پیام‌ها با اپل یا گوگل وارد شو.

شعرها و معنی‌ها از گنجور (ganjoor.net) هستن.

**Keywords:** شعر,حافظ,سعدی,مولانا,خیام,فردوسی,فال,مشاعره,دیوان,غزل,شاهنامه,بیت,ادبیات,معما,کلمه

## English (en)

**Name:** Yebeyt

**Subtitle:** Persian poetry, games, friends

**Promotional text:**
A new couplet every day from Hafez, Saadi, Rumi and Khayyam. Read, listen, play word games with the poems and hold poetry duels with friends.

**Description:**
Yebeyt («یه بیت», "one couplet") makes Persian poetry light and daily. The app is in Persian.

A couplet a day
- See today's couplet, keep it in your divan or pass for the next one.
- Take a Hafez fal, or find couplets by mood: love, longing, hope, spring…

Read and listen
- The Divan of Hafez, Saadi's Golestan and Bustan, Rumi's Masnavi and Divan-e Shams, Khayyam's quatrains, the Shahnameh and dozens more books by over forty poets.
- Read couplet by couplet or the whole poem, in the font and size you like.
- See what each couplet means.
- Listen to recitations; the text follows the voice.

Play
- Daily puzzle: find the missing word of a couplet.
- Couplet jigsaw: put the words back in place.
- Guess the poet, the second line, which book, match the meanings, and listen-and-pick.
- Earn points, keep your daily streak and climb the leaderboard.

With friends
- Find friends, send couplets and messages.
- Hold a mosha'ere (poetry duel), play live duels or send a challenge.
- Build a shared divan of the couplets you both love.

Reading, the library, fal and the solo games work without an account. Sign in with Apple or Google for scores, friends and messages.

Poems and meanings come from Ganjoor (ganjoor.net).

**Keywords:** hafez,rumi,saadi,khayyam,ferdowsi,farsi,poems,fal,divan,ghazal,shahnameh,word game,trivia,iran

## App Review notes (answers to the 1.0 rejection, guideline 2.1)

1. **Screen recording:** attached. It was recorded on an iPhone and goes from launch through reading, a game, Sign in with Apple, a friend request, a message, report and block, and deleting the account.
2. **Purpose and audience:** Yebeyt is a free app for reading and playing with classical Persian poetry, for Persian speakers of any age. It has no ads and no purchases, and it sells no data.
3. **How to reach the features:**
   - Reading, the library, search, fal, recitations and the solo games need no account: tabs خونه (home), کاوش (explore) and بازی (games).
   - Server features ask for an account at the moment they are used: like, scores, friends, messages and games with friends. Sign in with your own Apple ID or Google account; no demo login is needed.
   - **Friends and messages:** on the خونه tab (home), «دوستات» (friends) → «پیدا کردن دوست» (find friends), search `yebeyt`. That is the app's own demo friend: it accepts friend requests at once and answers messages with a couplet, so messaging can be tried with one account.
   - **Report and block:** open a person's page → ⋯ → «گزارش» (report) or «بلاک» (block).
   - **Delete the account:** من → تنظیمات (settings) → «پاک کردن حساب» (delete account) → confirm. It deletes the account and everything stored for it on the server.
4. **External services:**
   - Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In.
   - Our own server at api.yebeyt.com (hosted on Railway, EU).
   - Ganjoor's public poetry data (github.com/ganjoor) and recitation API (api.ganjoor.net).
   - Apple Push Notification service.
5. **Regional differences:** none. The app is the same in every country where it is offered.
6. **Rights to third-party material:**
   - The poems are classical works in the public domain.
   - The meanings, summaries and recitations come from Ganjoor, which publishes them openly; Ganjoor is credited in the app and on yebeyt.com. Ganjoor's written permission is attached (waiting for its reply; see owner-todo §4).
   - The fonts are under open licences, listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.
